`tailscale` `networking` `influxdb` `kubernetes`

## Context

VPS-hosted applications needed access to homelab datastores without adding another proxy or gateway layer.

## Changes

Extended the existing Tailscale subnet routing through `svc-utility` to selected Kubernetes MetalLB endpoints, including ArangoDB, InfluxDB, and `datastore-api`. The latter allows access to MySQL, Postgres and Redis as they do not natively have HTTP access.

Restored persistent IPv4 forwarding on the Alpine `svc-utility` subnet router by enabling the OpenRC `sysctl` service at boot.

Validated direct VPS access to InfluxDB through the routed MetalLB endpoint. Confirmed InfluxDB 1.12.4 authentication and successful queries against the `ai-sandbox` health dataset.

## Result

The VPS can directly reach selected homelab datastore APIs over Tailscale. InfluxDB requires no additional proxy layer, while `datastore-api` remains available for services that require an application gateway.
