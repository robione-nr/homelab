## Continuous Unison Operation

Stabilized the OpenRC-managed Unison synchronization service under the `admin` user. The service explicitly supplied `HOME`, `USER`, and `LOGNAME`, and used the available Unison fsmonitor helper so continuous synchronization did not depend on an interactive shell environment.

Bidirectional synchronization was functioning, but broken SSH pipes and delayed/watch behavior remained operational concerns. Logs sometimes reflected previously completed work or grouped activity in ways that made it difficult to determine whether a new editor save had actually propagated.

## Result

The service-management pattern itself was sound: Unison could run continuously under OpenRC with a non-root service identity and synchronize the CephFS/VPS trees.

The remaining problem shifted from “can Unison synchronize?” to “can the continuous watcher be trusted to notice the file-change patterns produced by real editors?” That distinction later drove more focused validation and consolidation work.
