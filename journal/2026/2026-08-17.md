## AI Service Platform

Consolidated the Kubernetes AI platform around Hermes on `p100-01`, llama.cpp inference, Supermemory, PostgreSQL/pgvector, ArangoDB, Mattermost, Obsidian/Git, and CephFS. Each component had a distinct responsibility instead of treating “AI memory” or “agent state” as one undifferentiated database.

Internal services were standardized behind Cilium LoadBalancer addresses in the `10.5.0.x` range. Kubernetes VMs were changed to expose host CPU capabilities where required, resolving AVX-dependent workload failures. ArangoDB reachability was also corrected through the L2 service configuration.

## Result

The environment became a platform rather than a collection of experiments: model serving, collaboration, graph data, vector retrieval, durable memory, and shared files could be addressed as separate services.

That separation later made it possible to assign narrower permissions and clearer ownership to Hermes agents instead of giving every agent direct access to every datastore.
