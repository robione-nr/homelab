## Proxmox Remote Administration

Brought Proxmox and Tailscale together as the practical remote-management foundation for the homelab. Tailscale was installed directly on the Proxmox host rather than placed in a container so management connectivity would remain available independently of guest state.

Persistence across reboot was verified, another machine was added to the tailnet, and SSH connectivity was tested. This was particularly important because much of the homelab work was being performed remotely between Texas and North Dakota; losing the management path could turn a routine network change into an onsite recovery requirement.

## Result

The tailnet became the dependable administrative overlay while the physical LAN, addressing, switching, and cluster architecture were still changing. It allowed infrastructure work to continue without making public exposure of Proxmox or SSH part of the design.

This management path later supported the more complex VPS↔homelab integrations, although application and replication traffic would still be designed deliberately rather than assuming Tailscale was the answer to every network path.
