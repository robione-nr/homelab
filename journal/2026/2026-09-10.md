## Ming Text, Image, and Video Port

Advanced the Ming-flash-omni-2.0 llama.cpp port from a working text backbone to functional text, image, and video input on `p100-01`. The port used the BailingMoeV2 language model together with Ming-specific multimodal routing and projector support rather than reducing the model to text-only operation.

Vision-capable and Omni GGUF artifacts were produced along with vision-only and combined vision/audio projectors. The final quantized Omni LLM was Q3_K_L even though its retained filename still referenced `q3_k_m`, so artifact names were no longer treated as authoritative metadata.

Image input was validated successfully. Video ingestion also worked end-to-end: frames were decoded, encoded through the Ming vision path, supplied to the LLM, and produced a coherent chronological description. Multiple-media session behavior was tested across image/video sequences, with `/clear` required between independent tests to prevent prior media from remaining in the active prompt.

## Resource Constraint

The combined BF16 projector could not fit comfortably on GPU 0 alongside the distributed LLM. Running with `--no-mmproj-offload` kept the projector on CPU, trading speed for reliable VRAM usage across the four 16 GB P100s.

## Remaining Work

Audio input became the focused unresolved modality. Text, 64K context, image, and video were treated as known-good baselines unless a regression appeared. The remaining work centered on Ming's audio encoder/projector/router path rather than general multimodal compatibility.
