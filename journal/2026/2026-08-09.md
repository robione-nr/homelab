## Dedicated 10GbE Storage Fabric

Moved the network toward `10.0.1.0/24` for general infrastructure and `10.10.10.0/24` for dedicated Ceph traffic. Proxmox hosts used their 1GbE and 10GbE interfaces for distinct traffic classes rather than allowing storage replication to compete freely with management and application traffic.

The Arista 7050 MLAG peer network was moved to `172.31.255.0/30`. This removed an address-space overlap with Kubernetes and made the switch-internal peer relationship clearly separate from routable service networks.

## Result

The network now had explicit functional boundaries: ordinary infrastructure traffic, high-bandwidth Ceph traffic, Kubernetes address space, and switch peer communication no longer depended on ambiguous overlapping ranges.

This separation made later troubleshooting easier because an address implied its intended role, and it reduced the chance that a future Kubernetes or service CIDR expansion would collide with physical-network infrastructure.
