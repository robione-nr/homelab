## Storage and AI Host Direction

Defined the Supermicro SYS-1028GQ-TXR with four Tesla P100 SXM2 GPUs as the dedicated AI compute system alongside the Dell PowerEdge infrastructure. The machine provided a clear home for large local inference workloads that would otherwise compete with general virtualization services.

Storage planning continued to favor exposing SSDs individually instead of combining them behind a large hardware RAID virtual disk. The immediate configuration could remain simple, but the important constraint was preserving the ability to hand devices directly to ZFS or Ceph later.

## Decision

The GPU server would participate in the broader Proxmox/homelab environment while retaining a distinct compute role. Likewise, storage decisions were evaluated against the future distributed architecture rather than only immediate convenience.

This avoided two expensive reversals: rebuilding storage because Ceph could not see individual devices, and redesigning the compute environment because AI workloads had been mixed indiscriminately with persistent infrastructure.
