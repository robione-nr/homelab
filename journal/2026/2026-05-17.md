## Private Connectivity and Service Integration

Installed Tailscale on the NAS/Xubuntu system and verified it online. This created a practical private path between the home environment and the VPS despite Starlink/CGNAT constraints, avoiding the need to expose internal administrative services directly to the public Internet.

The work was part of a broader integration effort involving Mattermost, MySQL, Ollama, and AI-agent services. The personal website architecture was also beginning to reflect the homelab itself, with `/lab/`, `/agents/`, API endpoints, and live infrastructure status considered as public-facing views into systems running elsewhere.

## Design Direction

Tailscale was treated as an infrastructure transport rather than a user-facing feature. Public web traffic could continue terminating on the VPS while selected backend services remained private and reachable over the tailnet. That pattern later became important for database replication, local inference access, and other VPS-to-homelab service paths.

The separation also clarified that the web projects and homelab were not independent projects: public applications could live on the VPS while depending on private compute and data services hosted at home.
