## Redundant Network Design

Defined the physical network around two FortiGate 201E firewalls, two Arista 7010T-48 switches for general Ethernet traffic, and two Arista 7050SX-64 switches for the 10GbE/storage fabric. The three Dell R630s would be dual-connected so loss of a single switch or link would not isolate a virtualization host.

The FortiGates were intended to operate as the shared gateway/security plane using HA. The Arista switches would remain independently managed, with redundancy created through topology and link design rather than assuming one large logical switch was required everywhere.

## Decision

Resiliency was prioritized over maximizing aggregate throughput. A predictable surviving path after a device or link failure was more valuable than using every interface solely to increase peak bandwidth.

This physical design provided the basis for the later separation between general infrastructure traffic and dedicated Ceph traffic, and for subsequent MLAG/VLAN work once onsite recovery access was practical.
