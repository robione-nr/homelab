## Ming GGUF Conversion

Converted the text path of InclusionAI Ming-flash-omni-2.0 into GGUF for the quad-P100 environment. BF16 and quantized artifacts were produced as part of determining whether the model could be made practical on the available hardware.

The llama.cpp runtime context was extended beyond the earlier 32K limitation, and retrieval was validated with a prompt exceeding 56K tokens. This was important because the homelab AI design favors long-running jobs and large context rather than optimizing only for short interactive prompts.

## Limitation

Text inference was functional, but Ming's multimodal architecture required additional projector/runtime support for image, video, audio, and generation paths. A successful text conversion therefore did not imply that the full any-to-any model was supported.

## Result

The experiment established a working local text path and demonstrated that the runtime/context configuration could support materially larger prompts. Subsequent work focused on the multimodal projector and llama.cpp compatibility rather than repeating the base model conversion.
