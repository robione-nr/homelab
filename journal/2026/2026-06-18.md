## Homelab Repository Established

Created the dedicated homelab documentation effort to capture architecture, diagrams, projects, operational decisions, and lessons learned. By this point the rack environment was no longer conceptual: servers were integrated into the LAN, Proxmox and initial guests were operating, Tailscale remote access had been verified, and the VPS continued hosting web, MySQL, email, and the Utility Server.

The repository was intended to document the system as an evolving platform rather than become a collection of installation tutorials. That meant recording why components were selected, how responsibilities were divided, and what the resulting architecture looked like after meaningful changes.

## Result

The homelab repository became the durable reference point for infrastructure work. Conversational troubleshooting and terminal history could remain ephemeral; stable architecture, decisions, and milestones belonged in Git.

This journal is a continuation of that decision: reconstruct enough context to understand the evolution of the system without preserving the enormous volume of intermediate troubleshooting that produced it.
