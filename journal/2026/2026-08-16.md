## Homelab Web Serving Path

Validated a Kubernetes web-serving stack using a shared Cilium ingress VIP with site content backed by CephFS. This allowed VPS-hosted projects to participate in a homelab serving/failover design instead of existing only as isolated files under the VPS Apache installation.

`dexanearme.co` and `apexdigital.llc` were among the domains exercised through the path. TLS, ingress routing, storage visibility, and domain handling therefore became cluster concerns rather than one-off per-site configuration.

## Architectural Effect

The web projects became explicit workloads of the homelab architecture. Source/project content could be synchronized between environments while the serving layer remained independently replaceable.

This also clarified why web work belongs in the infrastructure journal: changes to CephFS, ingress, DNS, TLS, Apache, synchronization, or backend services directly determine whether the sites operate, even when the visible result is simply a website.
